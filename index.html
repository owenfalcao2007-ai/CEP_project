About Us

Rooted in Community, Growing for the Planet

How We Began

Whispering Green Foundation started in 2014 as a small group of neighbors picking up litter on a local beach. What began as a weekend habit grew into a nationwide movement of volunteers, educators, and changemakers united by one belief: that small, consistent action creates lasting environmental change.

Today we run plastic waste management drives, tree plantation programs, and school awareness campaigns across 30+ cities — but our heart remains the same grassroots spirit we started with.

Our Vision

A world where every community lives in harmony with nature — where clean beaches, thriving forests, and responsible consumption are the norm, not the exception.

  • Pollution-free coastlines and rivers by 2035
  • A generation of environmentally literate youth
  • Circular, zero-waste local economies

Our Mission

To mobilize communities through hands-on environmental action, education, and sustainable innovation — reducing plastic waste, restoring green cover, and building lasting eco-conscious habits.

  • Organize large-scale cleanup and plantation drives
  • Educate students and communities on sustainability
  • Partner with local bodies for lasting waste solutions

Core Values

  • Integrity — Transparent use of every donated rupee
  • Community First — Locally-led, locally-owned solutions
  • Hope in Action — Optimism backed by measurable results
  • Inclusivity — Environmental action for everyone, everywhere

Contributing to the UN Sustainable Development Goals

Every initiative we run is mapped to global sustainability targets — connecting local action to worldwide impact.

Sustainable Cities & Communities

We help cities manage waste responsibly and expand accessible green public spaces for all residents.

  • Urban tree plantation & green corridors

  • Clean public spaces through regular cleanups
